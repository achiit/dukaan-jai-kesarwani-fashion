About the shop

A family shop on the highway, for the families around it

JK
The Kesharwani BrothersOwners, Jai Kesharwani Brothers

Hum bhai milkar yeh dukaan chalate hain. Hamare pitaji ne kapde ka kaam shuru kiya tha, aur humne unse sabse pehle yahi seekha ki grahak ka bharosa kapde se bhi zyada zaroori hai.

Aaj bhi koi shaadi ka joda dhoondhne aata hai to hum use ghar ke ek member ki tarah dikhate hain. Pehle sabko baithate hain, phir kapde dikhate hain, phir daam ki baat.

Gopiganj ke log aur G.T. Road se guzarne wale musafir, dono ko yahan se saaf daam aur sahi naap ke saath kapda milta hai. Yahi hamari kamai hai.
